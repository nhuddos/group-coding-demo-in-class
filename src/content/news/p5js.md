p5.js is a really cool JavaScript library that makes creative coding easy and fun. It lets you draw, animate, and play with visuals right in the browser — perfect for artists and beginners alike.

With just a few lines of code, you can turn ideas into interactive art. It’s open-source, beginner-friendly, and endlessly inspiring!

👉 Learn more at [the p5js site](https://p5js.org)
